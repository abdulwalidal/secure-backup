3. Zero-Knowledge Cryptography & User Responsibility

Secure Backup operates on a strict Zero-Knowledge Architecture:

  • Passphrase Ownership: All encryption keys are derived directly from the passphrase you supply using Argon2id. Secure Backup has no central server, no master recovery key, and no backdoor.
  • Irrecoverable Data Loss: If you forget, lose, or misplace your backup passphrase, it is mathematically impossible for the developers or any third party to recover or decrypt your backup files. You acknowledge that you bear sole and exclusive responsibility for backing up and safeguarding your passphrases.
