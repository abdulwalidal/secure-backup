Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

1. Overview & Architecture

Secure Backup is an open-source, client-side desktop backup and encryption application developed by Abdul Wali Dal. The software is distributed under the MIT License and operates entirely locally on your personal desktop computer.

We do not operate any proprietary servers, telemetry trackers, advertisement networks, or cloud databases. All cryptographic operations (passphrase hashing, key derivation, AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption, and SQLite catalog transactions) take place strictly on your local device.

2. Information We Access and Process

Because Secure Backup is client-side desktop software, the authors and maintainers of Secure Backup do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal data or file contents. Specifically:

  • Local Files & Backups: Selected folders are read directly from your filesystem and encrypted with AES-256-GCM prior to archive generation. Your plaintext files never leave your machine.
  • Passphrases & Keys: Passphrases are processed in local memory using Argon2id with random salts. Passphrases and derived keys are never logged, written to disk, or sent across any network.
  • Local Database: Backup snapshot history, file sizes, SHA-256 integrity hashes, and cloud sync records are stored solely in a local SQLite file (~/.local/share/secure-backup/backup.db) on your computer.

3. Google User Data & Google Drive Integration

Secure Backup offers optional cloud synchronization to your personal Google Drive storage. When you choose to connect your Google account:

  • Authentication: Authentication occurs via desktop OAuth 2.0 with Proof Key for Code Exchange (PKCE) over a temporary local loopback server (http://127.0.0.1:8080). Your Google password is never seen or collected by Secure Backup.
  • OAuth Scopes Requested: Secure Backup requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope along with basic profile identification (userinfo.email, openid).
  • Scope Limitations: The drive.file scope restricts our application so that it can only access, create, and modify files and folders that Secure Backup itself has created (specifically, the isolated Secure Backup Vault folder). Secure Backup cannot and does not access, view, modify, or delete any of your other personal Google Drive files, photos, or documents.
  • Token Storage: OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens received from Google are stored exclusively in your local SQLite ledger with restricted operating system permissions (chmod 600). Tokens are never shared with or transmitted to any third party.
  • Uploaded Data: All files uploaded to Google Drive are encrypted ciphertext (.enc) and structured JSON manifests. Even Google cannot view the plaintext content of your backups without your client-side passphrase.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Limited Use Requirements: Secure Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • Google user data is used solely to facilitate user-initiated backup synchronization and disaster recovery catalog discovery.
  • We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties, advertising platforms, data brokers, or external cloud services.
  • We do not use Google user data to serve advertisements or train machine learning/AI models.
  • Humans are not permitted to read Google user data or uploaded files.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

You maintain complete control over your cloud integrations and local data at all times:

  • Disconnecting Accounts: You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from within the application's Settings tab. Disconnecting immediately deletes your access and refresh tokens from your local database.
  • Revoking Permissions: You can revoke Secure Backup's access to your Google account at any time through Google Account Permissions.
  • Deleting Cloud Files: You can delete any uploaded snapshot from your Google Drive by deleting the Secure Backup Vault folder directly in Google Drive or within the app interface.
