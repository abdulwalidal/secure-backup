Zero-Knowledge Desktop Backup

An open-source desktop backup system engineered in Rust, Tauri 2, and TypeScript. All files are encrypted with authenticated AES-256-GCM locally before optional synchronization to personal cloud vaults.

Client-Side Zero-Knowledge

Your passphrase never leaves your machine. Keys are derived via Argon2id with cryptographically random salts, producing authenticated AES-256-GCM ciphertexts.

Personal Google Drive Sync

Connect your personal Google account via OAuth 2.0 PKCE loopback servers to sync encrypted archives directly into your own private Google Drive storage vault.

Disaster Recovery Catalog

If your local hard drive is wiped or corrupted, Secure Backup discovers remote snapshot manifests from your cloud vault and restores your snapshot ledger cleanly.

100% Free & Open Source

Transparent open-source architecture with zero proprietary lock-in, zero telemetry, and zero third-party relay servers. Licensed under the MIT License.

Cloud Integration Purpose: Secure Backup connects directly from your local desktop client to Google APIs via standard OAuth 2.0 PKCE. It only requests permission to manage its own isolated backup vault folder (drive.file scope). No unencrypted files or user credentials ever touch third-party servers.
